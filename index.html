By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To facilitate company registration and administrative processing, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification Information: Full legal name, email address, phone number, residential or postal address, and national identification or passport details required for director/shareholder verification.
  • Business & Registration Data: Proposed company names, business category, ownership structure, director/shareholder details, tax identification numbers, and uploaded registration documents.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

  • Processing, verifying, and submitting your company registration applications.
  • Communicating application status updates, approvals, and administrative notices.
  • Creating and maintaining your user account within the App.
  • Providing customer support and responding to inquiries.
  • Complying with legal, regulatory, and statutory business registration requirements.
  • Improving application performance, security, and user experience.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share data under the following circumstances:

  • Cloud Infrastructure & Backend Services: We utilize Google Firebase (such as Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database / Firestore) to securely store user data, authenticate accounts, and sync application records.
  • Regulatory & Registration Authorities: Where necessary to complete your company registration request, submitted corporate details are shared with relevant statutory registration bodies.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS in transit and encrypted cloud storage at rest) to protect your personal and corporate documents against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill your registration requests, maintain your active account, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data stored within the App. If you wish to delete your account and associated registration data, you may submit a deletion request using the contact details below. Upon verification, we will permanently remove your personal data from our active databases, except where retention is required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended strictly for business owners, entrepreneurs, and adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction).
